Our Process

The LensGlow Restoration Process

A systematic, multi-stage approach that delivers consistent, professional-grade results every time.

1. Inspection

Assess damage level, check for internal condensation, and determine the optimal restoration approach.

2. Wet Sanding

Progressive wet sanding from 800 to 3000 grit to remove the oxidized surface layer safely.

3. Compound Polish

Machine polishing with cutting compound followed by finishing polish for mirror-like optical clarity.

4. UV Seal

Apply UV ceramic coating to seal and protect the restored surface for years of crystal-clear performance.
