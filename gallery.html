Visual Stages

The Lens Clarity Journey

A microscopic view of how we treat polycarbonate material to bring dead lenses back to absolute clarity.

01

Oxidized / Yellow

Polycarbonate chains are broken down. Deep surface cracks scatter light and yellowing beams.

02

Shaved & Matte

Wet sanding removes oxidized plastic. Lenses look uniform, smooth, but frosted white and matte.

03

Buffed & Polished

Micro-abrasive polishing removes frosted matte. Mirror-like, factory-new optical clarity is restored.

04

Polymer Sealed

UV ceramic coating forms a hard, non-yellowing microscopic shield, preserving the clarity for years.
