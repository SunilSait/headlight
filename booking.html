Prepare for Results

Vehicle Preparation Checklist

To help our mobile technicians achieve factory-new clarity and complete your restoration quickly, please review this simple preparation checklist before your scheduled appointment slot.

1. Driveway Clearance

Ensure there is at least 3 feet of flat, unobstructed workspace around the front bumper of the vehicle for wet sanding machinery.

2. Clean Bumper Surface

While our technicians clean the lenses, a quick rinse of the front bumper helps prevent windblown dust contaminants during seal curing.

3. Key Handoff

We require the vehicle keys on-site only if we are testing internal high/low beam brightness levels or auxiliary fog assemblies.

4. No Car Wash for 24h

Do not run the vehicle through an automated high-pressure wash for 24 hours to allow the UV ceramic molecular shield to dry completely.

Important Curing Notice

Our UV ceramic coating uses solar-activated polymers. While our technicians fully cure the coating with high-intensity ultraviolet lamps on-site, the molecular shield continues to harden over the first 24 hours. Please avoid touching or scratching the lenses during this initial curing window.
