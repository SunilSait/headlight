Optical Physics

The Science of Headlight Degradation & Restoration

Understanding why headlights fade, and how our professional restoration process chemically cures polycarbonate lenses back to factory-new clarity.

1. Polycarbonate UV Degradation

Modern headlight lenses are manufactured from polycarbonate plastic. When exposed to solar UV radiation and atmospheric oxygen, the polymer chains break down, creating micro-cracks that scatter light and turn lenses yellow.

Phase: Oxidation

2. Micro-Refraction Recovery

Our technicians use progressive wet sanding grids (from 800 to 3000 grit) to physically shave off the microscopic layer of dead plastic. This is followed by polishing with cutting-edge micro-abrasives to align the lens surface.

Phase: Optical Alignment

3. UV-Crosslinked Polymer Seal

Cheap DIY kits leave plastic unprotected, causing yellowing to return within weeks. We apply an industry-grade UV-cured ceramic protective coat. Solar rays trigger cross-linked polymer bonding the clarity in for years.

Phase: Molecular Shield
